Outcome 3: Number of Participants With Worst Case Hematology Results by Maximum Grade Increase Post-baseline Relative to Baseline
Description: Blood samples were collected for the analysis of following hematology parameters: anemia, neutrophil count decreased and white blood cell decreased.
